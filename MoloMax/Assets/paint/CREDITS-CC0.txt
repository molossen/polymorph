Molo Paint starter pack

80 brush alphas (alphas/) and 80 relief textures (relief/) come from the SubCraft starter pack
by Aalzard (https://github.com/Aalzard/subcraft), dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

For Polymorph the alphas were resized to 256 px and the normal maps were turned into
512 px height maps (relief). No attribution is required; this note is a courtesy.
